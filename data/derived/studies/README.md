data/derived/studies/
---------------------
Derived study-level inputs and simulation objects used in analyses.

Contents
--------
- `*_n.csv`        Sample size or transition frequency inputs per study.
- `*_sim.RData`    Simulated study objects.
- `_data_sources.csv`  Bibliographic sources for studies and species.
- `studies.zip`    Archive of study inputs for sharing or backup.
